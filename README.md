# Free AI Business Builder

A browser-first AI content and business operating system.

## Goals
- Research content opportunities
- Generate original scripts and content plans
- Repurpose ideas into YouTube, Shorts and TikTok formats
- Track publishing and revenue targets
- Keep a truth/compliance ledger
- Run without paid AI APIs by default

## Important
Revenue targets are targets, not guarantees. Public publishing, account actions, spending and other consequential actions remain approval-gated.
